Google Algorithm Updates: Complete History and How to Adapt

A comprehensive guide to major Google algorithm updates and how they've changed SEO over the years.

Google makes thousands of algorithm updates every year. Major updates that have significantly impacted SEO include: Panda (2011) — targeted low-quality content. Penguin (2012) — targeted spammy backlinks. Hummingbird (2013) — improved understanding of search intent. RankBrain (2015) — AI-powered search results. BERT (2019) — better natural language understanding. Core Web Vitals (2021) — page experience signals. Helpful Content Update (2022) — rewards people-first content. To adapt, focus on creating high-quality, user-focused content and following SEO best practices.
