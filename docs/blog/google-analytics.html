Google Analytics for SEO: How to Track and Measure Your SEO Performance

Learn how to set up and use Google Analytics to track your SEO performance, user behavior, and conversion metrics.

Google Analytics is essential for measuring the success of your SEO efforts. Set up goals and conversions to track important actions. Monitor organic traffic trends over time. Analyze which pages and keywords drive the most traffic. Study user behavior metrics like bounce rate, session duration, and pages per session. Use UTM parameters to track campaigns. Set up custom reports and dashboards. Connect Google Analytics with Google Search Console for deeper insights.
