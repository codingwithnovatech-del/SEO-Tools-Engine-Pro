LinkedIn SEO: How to Optimize Your Profile and Content for LinkedIn Search

Learn how to optimize your LinkedIn profile and posts to rank higher in LinkedIn search and build your professional brand.

LinkedIn has its own search algorithm that determines who finds your profile and content. To optimize: Use relevant keywords in your headline, about section, and experience. Complete all sections of your profile. Publish regular content on LinkedIn. Engage with others' content strategically. Build a strong network in your industry. Get recommendations and endorsements. Join and participate in relevant LinkedIn groups.
